Executive summary

Your goal. US$5,000 a month in today's money from age 55. Your spouse covers US$1,500, so your investments must pay US$3,500 a month, which needs about US$1.2 million at 55.

Where you stand. You hold US$71k across eToro and your Skandia pension plan. Risk is already at the agreed level, but the mix leans on US technology and 13% sits in cash.

Our recommendation. A diversified, value-led portfolio expected to earn 8.0% a year. In a 2008-type crisis it could fall about 45%, so we lower its risk gradually from age 45.

Will it be enough? With today's savings the goal is reached in 72 of 100 scenarios, below the 80 we aim for. A weaker peso is the main risk you cannot control.

Three decisions for you:

  1. Put new savings and the excess cash into value shares and gold, not more technology.
  2. Save US$1,700 a month and retire at 56: success rises to 82 in 100 (90 if you retire at 59).
  3. Keep Skandia as it is, review the plan every April and start reducing risk at 45.

Methodology

  • Nine course asset classes plus a cash class (US T-bill proxy at the course risk-free rate, 4.0%).
  • Capital-market assumptions: course blend (50% 30-year history + 50% J.P. Morgan 2026 LTCMA).
  • Constrained efficient frontier; recommended portfolio at 12% volatility, de-risking to 8% between ages 45 and 55.
  • Monte Carlo with annual steps (10,000 paths): asset classes plus a real US$/peso factor; Skandia fees and FX exposure modelled explicitly; US$42,000 a year withdrawn from 55.
  • Risk: parametric and historical VaR/ES, 2008 and COVID replays, FX sensitivity and contribution/retirement-age levers.

Data: Bloomberg monthly total-return indices (course workbook, to Apr-2026); course assumptions (J.P.
